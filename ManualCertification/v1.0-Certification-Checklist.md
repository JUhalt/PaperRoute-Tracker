# v1.0 certification checklist

One ordered list of everything the 1.0 certification ([#313](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/issues/313)) asks for. It is gathered from the [standing procedure](Release-Certification.md), from [v0.9.2's checklist](v0.9.2-Certification-Checklist.md), which it copies and adds to (step 9), and from what changed since v0.9.2. It is grouped by what has to be set up, so that each setting is changed once: the host first, then the rc's publication, then one session on the certification VM, then Stable, then the close-out.

**How to use it.** Work down the list in order. C6 and C7 are the exceptions: they run on a build of master before B1 (decisions 16 and 22). So is the VM: the maintainer builds it while C runs, and D0 checks it before D1. A runs in the order #313 lists it: A1, A2, A9, A10 and A11, then the rc prep (A4 to A6), then A3, A7 and A8 at the release commit.
- Decision numbers are #313's. Its **Decisions** section records each answer, settled on November 2, 2026 or before, and the readings of them the maintainer confirmed that day.
- release.yml's line numbers are those at 404af9a. PR #314 adds 23 lines after line 24, so once it merges, each cited line from 25 on is 23 further down.
- Each line names where it comes from and what a pass looks like. Replace its `Result: not run` with what happened: pass or fail, the numbers, the evidence file names, or Narrator's words.
- A line that *carries over* gives, in its Result, the line it carries and that line's date: a #147 line, or for Stable an rc line. The procedure asks that of any step a certification skips.
- Post results to #313 as they come. The issue holds each result once (step 9).
- Evidence JSON is preferred to screenshots (step 10).
- Host evidence goes in `TestResults\v10-certification-evidence`, and guest evidence on the VM's evidence share. No evidence goes under `%TEMP%`.
- "E-results" is #147's record of its E, `TestResults\v092-certification-evidence\E-results.md`, and "E-results line N" is a line of it. 1.0's own record of E is `TestResults\v10-certification-evidence\E-results.md`.

**The issue,** #313, was opened on November 2, 2026 (decision 1). Step 11 says to open it when the release starts. 1.0 started when v0.9.2 was published, on October 30, 2026.

**The rc, then Stable** (decisions 4 and 19). As v0.9.0 shipped (#97):
- **The rc.** `v1.0.0-rc.1`, prepared as v0.9.0-rc.1 was (PR #104, 03c8533): `Version` 1.0.0-rc.1, `AssemblyVersion` and `FileVersion` 1.0.0.0, and notes in `docs/releases/1.0.0-rc.1.md`. release.yml publishes a tag with a hyphen as a prerelease on the Preview channel, and not as Latest (lines 38–45 and 354 at 404af9a). Its Unreleased gate runs only for Stable (line 55). A, B, C, D and E run in full for the rc.
- **Why an rc.** Only a tag run exercises vpk 1.2.161's download, pack and rewritten upload, which B1 cannot (#179's review; PR #309). The rc's tag is also the first run of A11's tag check, so a wrong refusal costs an rc tag, not `v1.0.0`.
- **Stable from the same commit** (decision 1). release.yml requires the vbproj's `Version` to equal the tag's (lines 66–71 at 404af9a), so `v1.0.0` cannot be tagged on the rc's own commit. The checklist reads "the same commit" as the same code, as the maintainer confirmed on November 2. The Stable prep (A12) changes only the `Version` line, the release documents and the bug template, and A12 checks that diff.
  - v0.9.0 also had two preps, PR #104 for its rc and PR #105 for Stable (#97). PR #105 carried Dark-theme fixes as well, in `GroupedListView.vb` and `UiPolish.vb`, with a new `DarkThemeLegibilityTests.vb`. So v0.9.0's Stable candidate ran 1037 tests to the rc's 1032, the five more being Dark-theme tests (#97). 1.0's Stable prep carries no code, and B5 runs the rc's test count.
- **When.** Stable starts once the rc's whole E has passed, E1 to E11, as the maintainer confirmed on November 2. Item 19 said "once E1/E2 pass on it". The Stable notes are fixed at B5, and say the Narrator, contrast and OneDrive checks ran on the rc, so the checklist waits for those too.
- **What runs again for Stable:** A12, B5 and B6 (the candidate and CI), D4 to D6 (publication), and E12 (Stable's install and update gates). B3, B4, C and E1 to E11 carry over from the rc, because the code is the rc's (A12, D6).

**Why the VM session comes after publication.** It is the same as for v0.9.2.
- Step 8 runs the installed-app gates on the published build, installed through the live updater or from the published Setup. It does not run them on a local candidate of the same commit.
- For 1.0, E runs on the published rc, whose code is 1.0.0's (A12, D6). So the checklist reads "1.0's installed build" in decisions 6 and 16, and in #276's acceptance, as the rc's, as the maintainer confirmed on November 2. E12 then runs the gates that depend on 1.0.0's own bytes: its Setup, its update package and its installed DLL.
- The maintainer's sign-off before the rc's tag is the gate. So everything that can be checked without a published build comes first, on the host.
- A line that fails after publication is recorded on the issue at once. A failure in the rc's E comes before the Stable prep, so its fix goes into an rc.2, certified as the rc was. After the Stable tag, the maintainer decides what follows, such as a 1.0.1 patch or withdrawing 1.0.0 from Stable, and #44 waits for that decision.
