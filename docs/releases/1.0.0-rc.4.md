# PaperRoute Tracker v1.0.0-rc.4 — Trusted Research Workflow

**Local-first, privacy-focused app for tracking academic manuscripts from draft through submission, revision, and publication.**

This Preview candidate is PaperRoute 1.0. It replaces v1.0.0-rc.3. Certification at a real 150% display found windows that cut text: six on rc.1, and one more on a second candidate, rc.2, which was not published. rc.3 fixed them, evened Delete Manuscript's margins at 150%, and let every list that scrolls sideways reach the end of its rows. Its own installed check at 150% then found that the lists you check still stopped a character or two short of a long row's end. rc.4 fixes that and changes nothing else. Like rc.1, it adds no new workflow to v0.9.2, the hardening release. It fixes what v0.9.2's certification found, polishes dialogs, brings the privacy policy and the User Guide up to date, and pins the build so that the release bundles exactly the .NET runtime it was tested with. Your library stays on Schema 9. v0.9.2 remains the current Stable release while installed, updater, and display certification is completed.

## What 1.0 means

1.0 is the release the project asks you to trust with your real publication pipeline. The [roadmap](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/ROADMAP.md#v10-trusted-research-workflow) sets the bar: library upgrades that keep your data, reliable installs and updates, recovery tools, a backup and restore proven to work, a keyboard-complete interface, Insights that says how each number is counted, manuscripts, submissions, decisions, revisions and versions that stay consistent with each other, clear privacy boundaries, and certified release files. The readiness record, [#44](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/issues/44), links the evidence for each part, from v0.9.2's certification and from 1.0's own, and 1.0 is published with the maintainer's sign-off on it; **Verification status** below says what is checked for this Preview. Accessible means what the User Guide's [Accessibility](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/docs/USER_GUIDE.md#accessibility) section says was tested, not a claim of conformance to an accessibility standard. What 1.0 knowingly leaves as it is, such as a 1366x768 screen at 150%, is listed under [Residual limitations](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/ROADMAP.md#residual-limitations).

## Changed since v1.0.0-rc.3

- **Long rows reach their very end.** rc.3 let every list that scrolls sideways reach the end of its rows, but on a real 150% display the lists you check still stopped a character or two short of a long row's end: Fill Blanks from Crossref showed a DOI as "10.5555/zzz-cert.0", with its final "3" out of reach. Windows has two ways of drawing text, and PaperRoute measured those rows with one and drew them with the other, which draws a long row slightly wider; the gap grew with the row, and was larger at 100% than at 150%. The lists you check, in Fill Blanks from Crossref, Check for Publications, Export and Import (BibTeX or RIS), ORCID Public Profile, Publication & CV Export, the reports' Include list and Add Manuscript Author's affiliations, now draw each row the way it is measured, so each scrolls a few pixels past the end of its longest row at 100%, 125% and 150%. Their text is very slightly narrower than before. In the other lists that scroll sideways, a row that holds a tab, such as a checklist item or an author's name pasted with one, now reaches its end too; at 100% it stopped 7 pixels or more short.

## Changed since v1.0.0-rc.1

- **Text whole at 150%.** rc.1's certification at a real 150% display found six windows that cut text, each unchanged since v0.9.2. The search fields on the Board, Library and Deadlines no longer cut the bottoms of letters such as g, p and y, at 100% too. The help icons in Submission Readiness, Record Journal Submission and Route View show their whole "?". Delete Manuscript's warning names everything the deletion removes instead of ending "editorial decisions,...". Map Spreadsheet Columns shows its column headings and its Map to choices whole. Add Author and Edit Author keep the Notes box above Save and Cancel, with every label on one line. In Add Manuscript Author, a long affiliation can be scrolled to its end. At 125%, Delete Manuscript now opens at its 100% layout scaled up, Map Spreadsheet Columns' two fixed columns and its heading row are a little wider and taller, and the help icons' "?" in those three windows is a little smaller.
- **Long rows reach their end.** At 150%, Fill Blanks from Crossref cut a long row at the list's edge, mid-DOI, with nothing to show that it went on, so "10.5555/demo.grit" read "10.555". The list now scrolls sideways to the end of the row. The other lists that scroll sideways already did, but Windows stopped short of a long row's end, most on a row with an "&": the lists you check in Check for Publications, Export and Import (BibTeX or RIS), ORCID Public Profile, Publication & CV Export and the reports' Include list, and the authors and versions in Manuscript Details, a journal's Readiness Checklist, Submission Readiness's requirements and the Submission Packet Vault's packets and files. Each now scrolls to the very end. Delete Manuscript keeps the same margin on its right as on its left at 150%.

## Changed since v0.9.2

- **Closing on a large library.** v0.9.2 could freeze its window for 18 to 30 seconds while closing a 500-manuscript library, and it held up Restore's restart and Windows shutdown the same way. 1.0 no longer lays the Board out again, card by card, as it closes.
- **Restore with your Documents folder in OneDrive.** OneDrive and Microsoft Defender briefly hold the files a restore has just written, and Restore could then stop with "access denied" and put your library back as it was. In v0.9.2's certification it did so in 2 of 5 attempts. A restore now waits for a held folder, for about ten seconds, before it gives up, and your library stays in its folder while it waits. That holds for **Restore Backup...** and for **Restore From Backup...** in the window PaperRoute shows when it cannot open your library, which now says what it is doing while it works.
- **Narrator and page fades.** With Windows' Animation effects on, Narrator read only the shortcut when Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+6 switched pages, said nothing on Alt+Left, and read "Collapse navigation" on the way back to the Board. A page switch now hands the keyboard straight to the page shown, and Narrator is told of it again once the fade ends. A manuscript opened from the Library, Deadlines or Insights now opens with the keyboard on its Overview tab.
- **The page fade shows.** On an ordinary laptop a page switch was a plain cut even with Animation effects on: the fade's picture was given to Windows in a way it shows nothing of, and was rarely ready in time. Pages now cross-fade over 120 ms, and a switch waits at most about 60 ms for the picture before it switches at once.
- **Dialogs open ready to type.** Readiness Notes opens with the keyboard at the end of its notes, not in the read-only requirement above them, and the read-only notes in Submission Details and the details in **PaperRoute could not start** open with none of their text selected.
- **Labels on their fields' line.** In New Submission Packet and Edit Submission Packet, the Packet name, Exact manuscript version, Readiness profile, Real journal submission and Revision round labels now line up with their fields at 100%, 125% and 150%; at 150% some sat up to 7 pixels off.
- **Frames and drop-downs.** Drop-down lists, the Board's filter and sort among them, are framed in Light as text boxes are, and show their value where Windows does. A box that scrolls is framed round its scroll bar, tables have the theme's quiet frame instead of a black one in Light and a white one in Dark, and the journal details card on Journals keeps its bottom border as it scrolls. No dialog shows Windows Forms' own icon or a Minimize button any more, and only a dialog you can resize shows Maximize. A footer's default button is spaced as evenly as the buttons beside it.
- **Nothing cut or out of line.** At 150%, Submission Details no longer cuts its empty Correspondence & Files hint at "…or drag files her": every empty list's hint is now drawn as it is measured, and an empty table's hint stays inside its frame. Labels, notes, check boxes and icons line up in Edit Packet File, the reminder, author, external link and affiliation editors, the checklist item editor, the cover letter's Abstract row, the bibliography import and Submission Readiness. A journal candidate's questions take only the lines they need. Publication & CV Export's toolbar no longer splits Select All from Select None. On Deadlines, a narrow row shortens the manuscript's title before it cuts the journal's name. The windows above are whole at 150%.
- **Plainer words.** When the title is all Paste a Title Page recognizes, its summary reads "Found: title" instead of one bare word, and Export BibTeX and Export RIS mark a filed manuscript "[File Drawer]", as the Library names the shelf, instead of "[FileDrawer]".
- **The .NET runtime it was tested with.** The build now uses one exact .NET SDK, 10.0.403, which the README names for building from source, so the release bundles the runtime its candidate was tested on: .NET 10.0.14, the runtime v0.9.2 also shipped with. v0.9.2's `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` named 10.0.12; 1.0's names 10.0.14, and a release whose notices name another runtime is refused.
- **The privacy and security policies.** The [privacy policy](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/PRIVACY.md) has one row for each online service, named as **Online services** in Preferences names it, with what each one sends, now including the id of the journal you pick for its facts, the ids of the journals Find journals finds, everything an AI assistant window can send, and, for **Test Connection**, only your key, if you added one. It now says that, apart from the update check at startup, each online feature runs only when you choose it. The [security policy](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/.github/SECURITY.md) links the privacy and code signing policies.
- **The User Guide** now names `Documents\PaperRoute Library`, where PaperRoute keeps its copies of your files; says to report a security problem privately; names the screens 1.0 is checked on where it talks about clipped windows; says that a Crossref, ORCID, BibTeX or RIS import stores a publication date given only as a year, or a year and month, as the first day of that year or month, which Insights counts as written; and says that the Narrator and contrast-theme checks run on each release's published build. **What Windows shows** says what Edge shows for the unsigned Setup, **Keep** and then **Keep anyway**, and that Windows may also show **Windows protected your PC**. Deadlines lists possible publications, the fifth kind of item. A new how-to says how to start fresh with an empty library and keep the old one, **Updates and the Preview channel** says that an install made with a Preview release's Setup starts on the Stable channel, and the developer build's folder, `Documents\PaperRoute Dev Library`, is named. **What Windows draws** no longer lists tables' white frame, and names drop-down lists' outlines with those of text boxes and lists.
- **Behind the scenes:** the build and release workflows use newer versions of GitHub's checkout (7.0.1), .NET setup (6.0.0) and artifact upload (7.0.2) actions, and the tests use MSTest 4.5.2. Velopack, which installs and updates PaperRoute, moves from 1.2.0 to 1.2.161, and each release candidate records the display-scaling mode the published app sets. The release workflow refuses a tag whose commit is not on master. The Anthropic SDK behind the optional AI assistant moves from 12.51.0 to 13.3.0, now under the Apache License 2.0, as the notices say, and PaperRoute turns its OpenTelemetry tracing off. The on-screen fade tests run SystemAware, as PaperRoute does, and the certification harness opens any Board shelf and refuses to capture a minimized window. The code signing policy records the application to the SignPath Foundation and its decline, the roadmap adds v1.2, Open Science Studies, the responsiveness record adds v0.9.2's times with the library folder in OneDrive, and the test suite's README lists every test file.

## Code signing

**PaperRoute 1.0 is not code-signed.** The project applied to the SignPath Foundation for free code signing for open-source projects on October 30, 2026. On October 31, 2026, SignPath declined for now: the Foundation program looks for signs of public adoption that a young project does not yet have, and welcomes a new application later ([#216](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/issues/216)). That issue, now planned for v1.1, holds the routes to try next: WinGet, a Microsoft Store (MSIX) build, or a new application once the project has external references. Until releases are signed, your browser and Windows may warn before Setup runs; the User Guide's [Installing](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/docs/USER_GUIDE.md#installing) section explains the warning and how to check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The [code signing policy](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/CODE_SIGNING.md) says what will be signed, how, and who approves each signing request.

## Before trying this candidate

1.0 uses the same **Schema 9** library as v0.9.0, v0.9.1 and v0.9.2 and migrates no data. It changes no library file's layout, so v0.9.2 can still open a library 1.0 has used; restore a backup made by 1.0 with v0.9.2 or later. Updating from v0.9.2 asks about unsaved edits first. To be offered this Preview, choose **Preview** under **Settings → Preferences... → Update channel**, and choose **Stable** again once v1.0.0 is out: while the channel is Preview, the Stable release is not offered. **From v0.9.0 or v0.9.1, save any open manuscript page before you choose Download & Restart:** those versions do not ask, so the edits would be lost. The [v0.9.2 release notes](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/docs/releases/0.9.2.md) describe the hardening you receive with 1.0 if you skipped it. If PaperRoute runs on more than one computer that shares a Documents folder, update every one of them before saving on any of them. Only the 0.9 releases are supported for updating straight to 1.0, and 1.0's certification runs the update from v0.9.2. An older version's update check offers 1.0 as well: the migration tests cover that update, but no release certification does, so create a backup with **Settings → Backup Library...** first. The [upgrade notes](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.0-rc.4/UPGRADE_NOTES.md) have the details.
