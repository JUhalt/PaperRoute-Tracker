# PaperRoute Tracker v0.7.0-rc.1 — Deadline Center

This Preview candidate replaces the Reminders page with **Deadlines**, one list of everything that needs action and when, and adds a publication check that runs only when you ask. v0.6.0 remains the current Stable release while installed, updater, and native display certification is completed.

## Included

- **Deadlines.** Revision deadlines, journal follow-ups, your reminders, unfinished submission preparation, and possible publications, grouped as Overdue, Today, Next 7 days, Later, No date, and Done. Counts across the top, chips for each kind, and a filter box. A revision shows how many reviewer comments are addressed. Work without a date stays visible instead of disappearing.
- **Act where you are.** Each row has its own actions: **Open** lands on the submission (at its reviewer responses for a revision), **Postpone...** offers tomorrow, a week, two weeks, or any date, **Done** completes a reminder, and **Clear Follow-up...** removes only the follow-up date. Every change is made on the record that owns the date, so Deadlines and the manuscript page always agree.
- **Always in view.** The rail's Deadlines badge counts what is overdue or due today, and **View in Deadlines →** sits beside Needs Attention on the Board. **Ctrl+4** opens Deadlines.
- **Check for Publications...** asks Crossref, and optionally an ORCID record, whether manuscripts you are tracking have been published: by DOI, by a preprint's link to its published version, or by title. Each possible match offers **Review Match**, **Mark Published...**, and **Ignore**. Nothing is checked in the background, and nothing changes until you choose.
- **Mark Published...** lists exactly what it will change before recording a normal lifecycle event. **Fill Blanks from Crossref...** fills empty publication fields after a preview and never replaces a value.
- **Fixes.** Revision deadlines name their journal. The first-deadline date in Add Manuscript shows in full at 150% scaling.

## Before trying this candidate

v0.7 upgrades the library to **Schema 7**, which remembers the possible publications you have reviewed. The upgrade validates your library and changes only the schema marker; manuscript data, managed files, and automatic backups stay byte-for-byte. **v0.6 cannot open a library after v0.7 has used it**, so make a portable backup with v0.6 first (**Settings → Backup Library...**) and keep it. The [upgrade notes](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/master/UPGRADE_NOTES.md) explain the details.
