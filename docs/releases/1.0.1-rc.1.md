# PaperRoute Tracker v1.0.1-rc.1 — Certification Follow-ups

**Local-first, privacy-focused app for tracking academic manuscripts from draft through submission, revision, and publication.**

This Preview candidate is PaperRoute 1.0.1, a patch for v1.0.0. It fixes five ways to lose work, each present since v0.6.0 or earlier: four concern people and affiliations in your author library, and one concerns text typed on the manuscript page. _PENDING All five were found on November 14, 2026, the day v1.0.0 was released, by reading the code; none is new in 1.0. _PENDING It also takes what 1.0's certification noted. It adds no window, and your library stays on Schema 9. v1.0.0 remains the current Stable release while this candidate is certified.

## Fixed

- **ORCID works imported from a manuscript page are kept.** On a manuscript page's Authors tab, **Manage Library** opens Authors & Affiliations. An ORCID works import started there, with **ORCID...**, did two things it should not have. It wrote the page's unsaved author changes to disk without **Save**, so **Discard** did not undo them on disk. And the next save of the library dropped the imported works, although "ORCID Import Complete" had said they were imported; they never appeared on the Board. This was present since v0.2.0. An import from **Library > Authors & Affiliations** was not affected, and neither was an import that applied only an iD, names or affiliations. An import started from the manuscript page now leaves the page's unsaved changes unsaved, and its imported works are kept and shown on the Board ([PR #355](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/pull/355)).
- **If you lost works this way,** import them again from **Library > Authors & Affiliations**. If you chose **Discard** after such an import, open that manuscript and check its Authors tab.
- **People a Crossref lookup added are kept until Save.** On a manuscript page, **DOI & Crossref Metadata...** can add new people and affiliations, which wait until you save the page. If **Submission Readiness...**, **Manage Library** or **Journal, Preprint & Links...** (closed with OK) was opened before **Save**, they were lost. The manuscript then listed them as "[Missing author record]", and no message appeared. This was present since v0.2.0, and through Submission Readiness since v0.4.0. People the lookup matched to someone already in your library were not affected, and neither was a lookup saved before any of those windows was opened. The people and affiliations a lookup added are now kept when any of those windows is opened before **Save**: Save writes them, and **Discard** drops them ([PR #355](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/pull/355)).
- **If a manuscript shows "[Missing author record]" this way,** remove those entries on its Authors tab and run the Crossref lookup again. The names and iDs the lookup supplied were never saved, so no backup holds them.
- **A BibTeX or RIS import keeps the people you just saved.** On a manuscript page, **Manage Library** opens Authors & Affiliations, which saves each change at once. If you added or changed people, ORCID iDs or affiliations there and then left the page with nothing to save, **Import BibTeX / RIS...** saved the main window's older copy of the author library over them. They were lost, and the import created those people again under new ids. This was present since v0.6.0. Changes made from **Library > Authors & Affiliations** were not affected, and neither were changes followed by the page's own **Save**. The import now reads the author library from disk first, and imports nothing if it cannot read it. _PENDING
- **A person the saved manuscript names can no longer be deleted from its page.** On a manuscript page, after you removed an author on the Authors tab without saving, **Manage Library** let you delete that person, or one of their affiliations, at once. **Discard** then left the saved manuscript naming a deleted record: for a person, its Authors tab showed "[Missing author record]" and the name, ORCID iD and affiliations were gone; for an affiliation, it was gone from the author. This was present since v0.2.0. A delete from **Library > Authors & Affiliations** was not affected. From the page, such a delete is now refused while the saved manuscript still names the person or affiliation. _PENDING
- **If people, iDs or affiliations went missing in either way,** `authors.bak`, beside `authors.json` in the data folder, is the author library as it was before its last save, so it still holds them if nothing has saved the author library since. A BibTeX or RIS import also put a copy of both files in the `backups` folder before it ran. The User Guide's [Recovering your library](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/docs/USER_GUIDE.md#recovering-your-library) says where these are. Otherwise restore a backup, or add the people again. _PENDING
- **What you type on the manuscript page is kept until you save.** A **Title** or **Target journal** typed on the Overview, or a **Current stage** chosen but not saved, could be silently replaced by the saved value, and **Save** then wrote the old value. It happened after **DOI & Crossref Metadata...** with **Title** left unticked; after a submission or decision step (**Add**, **Edit** or **Delete** a submission, a revision deadline, or Save's own Editorial Decision Required prompt); and after **Journal, Preprint & Links...** closed with OK. No message appeared. This was present since v0.2.0, and after a submission or decision step since v0.3.0. A page saved before any of those was not affected. What you typed or chose is now kept through them, unless the step itself sets the target journal or the stage. If you lost text this way, type it again: it never reached disk. _PENDING

## Changed

- **The Board's stage and sort filters.** Each step through the open stage list redrew the Board, which held the window on a large library. The Board now redraws for a stage or sort choice once, when the open list closes, with the shelves' drawing held while it does. A step with the arrow keys on the closed box still redraws at once. On a 500-manuscript library, in the test suite, three steps through the open list and then closing it took 392 to 583 ms, against 988 to 2,227 ms before, and one change from All stages to Submitted took 199 to 401 ms, against 555 to 768 ms ([PR #356](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/pull/356)).
- **The first online request after a resume.** After a virtual machine resumes from a saved state, the first online request could fail with "couldn't reach", as an update check did in 1.0's certification: PaperRoute's pool still held connections the network had dropped. A computer whose network drops connections without notice may meet the same. When a request that only reads and carries no content, such as an update check, gets no answer at all, PaperRoute now sends it once more, through the same Online services checks. A request with content, such as an AI assistant's text, is never sent twice. Whether one more try is enough after a real resume is checked on this candidate (see **Known limits**).
- **If your studies were on OSF.** A new User Guide section, [If your studies were on OSF](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/docs/USER_GUIDE.md#if-your-studies-were-on-osf), is for researchers who kept a study's plan, materials or data in an OSF project. It says what changes on OSF and when, with each fact linked to the Center for Open Science's own pages, what PaperRoute can hold for a study today, and the steps to take first.
- **Behind the scenes:** the repository holds WinGet manifest files for v1.0.0, under [packaging/winget](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/packaging/winget/README.md), checked by two tests; nothing has been submitted, and PaperRoute is not in WinGet's catalog. The official RO-Crate validator, roc-validator 0.12.1, found no REQUIRED failures in the RO-Crate metadata of a packet export PaperRoute wrote; the [record](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/ManualCertification/v0.9-RO-Crate-Evaluation/README.md) has the commands and counts. Two keyboard tests that failed while someone was using the PC now release the mouse buttons and modifier keys in the test thread's key state before they post a key, and a new test checks that each list that scrolls sideways measures again when its rows change.

## Known limits

- **A recovered draft after a Crossref lookup.** If the session ends without asking, for example when Windows shuts down, between a Crossref lookup that added people and **Save**, the recovered draft names people your author library does not have, and its Authors tab shows "[Missing author record]". This has been so since v0.9.2, and it is not fixed in 1.0.1; it is planned for a later release ([#361](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/issues/361)). Save the page after a lookup that adds people.
- **The first online request after a resume.** It is not yet shown that one more try is enough after a real resume ([#339](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/issues/339)).

## Code signing

**PaperRoute 1.0.1 is not code-signed.** The project's application to the SignPath Foundation for free code signing was declined on October 31, 2026, for want of signs of public adoption, and a new application is welcome later ([#216](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/issues/216)). That issue, planned for v1.1, holds the routes to try next: WinGet, a Microsoft Store (MSIX) build, or a new application once the project has external references. Until releases are signed, your browser and Windows may warn before Setup runs; the User Guide's [Installing](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/docs/USER_GUIDE.md#installing) section explains the warning and how to check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The [code signing policy](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/CODE_SIGNING.md) says what will be signed, how, and who approves each signing request.

## Before trying this candidate

1.0.1 uses the same **Schema 9** library as v0.9.0 to v1.0.0 and migrates no data. It changes no library file's layout, so v1.0.0 can still open a library 1.0.1 has used. Updating from v1.0.0 or v0.9.2 asks about unsaved edits first. **From v0.9.0 or v0.9.1, save any open manuscript page before you choose Download & Restart:** those versions do not ask, so the edits would be lost. To be offered this Preview, choose **Preview** under **Settings → Preferences... → Update channel**, and choose **Stable** again once v1.0.1 is out: while the channel is Preview, the Stable release is not offered. An install made with this Preview's Setup starts on the Stable channel. If PaperRoute runs on more than one computer that shares a Documents folder, update every one of them before saving on any of them. The [v1.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/docs/releases/1.0.0.md) describe what you receive with 1.0.1 if you are updating from v0.9.2. The [upgrade notes](https://github.com/JUhalt/PaperRoute-Tracker/blob/v1.0.1-rc.1/UPGRADE_NOTES.md) have the details.
